Frequently Asked

How early should we book? +
We recommend booking at least 6-8 months in advance, especially for dates during peak wedding seasons, to ensure our availability for your special day.
Do you travel for weddings? +
Yes, we love traveling! We cover weddings across India and internationally. Travel and accommodation expenses are billed additionally for out-of-station events.
Do you provide candid and traditional photography? +
Yes, our comprehensive packages include both candid photography (capturing natural moments) and traditional photography (for family group shots and rituals).
